==========================================================
OfferService - Phan Tich Chi Tiet
==========================================================

1. TONG QUAN
----------------------------------------------------------

OfferService quan ly viec soan va gui offer cho mot ho so ung tuyen.

No ho tro:
- tai man hinh sua offer
- luu nhap offer
- gui offer
- lap rap master data cho template, benefit, currency va manager

----------------------------------------------------------
2. PHU THUOC
----------------------------------------------------------

- `IApplicationRepository`
- `IOfferRepository`
- `IUserRepository`
- `IUnitOfWork`

Hang so ho tro:
- reporting manager roles = `HR`, `Manager`

----------------------------------------------------------
3. CAC PHUONG THUC CONG KHAI
----------------------------------------------------------

`GetOfferEditorAsync(string applicationId)`
- validate va tai application
- tai offer hien co neu ton tai
- xay dung DTO editor day du

`SaveDraftAsync(...)`
- uy quyen cho logic upsert dung chung voi `OfferStatus.Draft`

`SendOfferAsync(...)`
- uy quyen cho logic upsert dung chung voi `OfferStatus.Sent`

----------------------------------------------------------
4. LUONG GHI DU LIEU COT LOI
----------------------------------------------------------

`UpsertOfferAsync(...)`

Day la method trung tam cua service.

Nhung validation chinh:
- application phai ton tai
- application phai o trang thai co the soan/gui offer
- currency phai ton tai
- template id neu co phai parse duoc
- reporting manager id neu co phai parse duoc
- tat ca benefit id duoc chon phai parse duoc va ton tai

Hanh vi ghi du lieu:
- tao `ApplicationOffer` moi neu chua co
- chuan hoa cac text field tuy chon
- giu trang thai application dong bo voi `ApplicationStatus.Offer`
- dong bo lien ket benefit
- luu trong transaction ro rang
- tai lai du lieu moi nhat de tra response

----------------------------------------------------------
5. CAC HELPER QUAN TRONG
----------------------------------------------------------

`GetApplicationAsync(...)`
- helper lookup application dung chung voi bien the tracked/non-tracked

`CurrencyExistsAsync(...)`
- kiem tra ma currency voi master data trong repository

`SynchronizeBenefits(...)`
- dong bo add/remove o quan he nhieu-nhieu benefit cua offer

`BuildEditorDtoAsync(...)`
- lap rap payload editor day du
- gom tom tat application, du lieu offer co the sua va danh muc master data

`NormalizeOptionalText(...)`
- trim text nullable va doi chuoi chi co khoang trang thanh `null`

----------------------------------------------------------
6. GHI CHU THIET KE
----------------------------------------------------------

- `actorId` hien dang co trong method signature nhung chua duoc dung
- `BuildEditorDtoAsync` co chu y cap cac gia tri mac dinh hop ly khi offer chua ton tai
- Service dang dat viec lap rap UI gan voi logic ghi domain; hien tai tien, nhung ve sau co the to dan

----------------------------------------------------------
7. GHI CHU VE KHA NANG DUNG CHUNG
----------------------------------------------------------

`NormalizeOptionalText(...)` xuat hien o nhieu service va la ung vien tot de tach thanh helper chuan hoa text dung chung.
