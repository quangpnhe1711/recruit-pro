==========================================================
ManagerAnalyticsService - Phan Tich Chi Tiet
==========================================================

1. TONG QUAN
----------------------------------------------------------

ManagerAnalyticsService xay dung dashboard phan tich tuyen dung danh cho manager.

No tong hop du lieu tu:
- applications
- interviews
- jobs
- departments

Output la mot `ManagerRecruitmentAnalyticsDto` duy nhat, gom:
- overview metrics
- chuoi xu huong theo thang
- funnel
- phan bo pipeline
- hieu suat theo department
- bang tong hop theo department

----------------------------------------------------------
2. NGUON DU LIEU
----------------------------------------------------------

- `IApplicationRepository`
- `IInterviewRepository`
- `IJobRepository`

Service nay nang ve doc du lieu va dieu phoi.
No khong ghi du lieu.

----------------------------------------------------------
3. LUONG CHINH
----------------------------------------------------------

`GetRecruitmentAnalyticsAsync()`

Nhung buoc lon:
1. xac dinh cua so bao cao
- moc thang hien tai
- span 6 thang bat dau tu 5 thang truoc

2. tai metric thô theo chuoi song song logic
- status counts
- average review cycle
- active candidates
- pending interviews
- monthly applications
- monthly completed interviews
- department pipeline snapshot
- review cycles by department
- department open roles

3. tinh KPI cap cao hon
- offer acceptance rate
- metric chenh lech
- tong theo stage
- tong phan bo

4. lap rap cac vung view-model
- overview
- bieu do trend
- funnel
- distribution
- bang department

----------------------------------------------------------
4. CAC HELPER QUAN TRONG
----------------------------------------------------------

`BuildFunnelItem(...)`
- doi so luong thô thanh mot dong funnel co ty le tren tong da ung tuyen

`BuildDistributionItem(...)`
- tinh ty le tren tong cho cac card phan bo theo stage

`CalculatePercentDelta(...)`
- xu ly ca edge case khi ky truoc bang 0

----------------------------------------------------------
5. GHI CHU THIET KE
----------------------------------------------------------

- Service nay bien snapshot tu repository thanh gia tri san sang cho UI
- Nhieu phep lam tron dung `MidpointRounding.AwayFromZero`
- Logic uu tien default ben bi thay vi nem loi

----------------------------------------------------------
6. Y TUONG CAI TIEN
----------------------------------------------------------

- Mot so phep tinh ty le/chenh lech tong quat co the dua ve analytics utility dung chung
- Neu dashboard bat dau cham, day la ung vien tot de them caching
