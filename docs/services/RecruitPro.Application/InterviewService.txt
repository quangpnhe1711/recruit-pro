==========================================================
InterviewService - Phan Tich Chi Tiet
==========================================================

1. TONG QUAN
----------------------------------------------------------

InterviewService quan ly danh sach interview, sap lich, cap nhat trang thai va xoa.

No cung cung cap du lieu xay lich de UI su dung khi bo tri buoi phong van.

----------------------------------------------------------
2. PHU THUOC
----------------------------------------------------------

- `IInterviewRepository`
- `IApplicationRepository`
- `IUserRepository`
- `IUnitOfWork`

Service nay cung dua vao:
- `DbDateTime`
- `ApplicationStatusWorkflow`

----------------------------------------------------------
3. NANG LUC CHINH
----------------------------------------------------------

`GetInterviewsAsync(...)`
- danh sach phan trang cho HR/admin voi bo loc keyword, status va ngay

`GetCandidateInterviewsAsync(Guid userId)`
- tong hop interview tu cac ho so cua chinh ung vien

`GetScheduleDataAsync(string? applicationId)`
- tra ve tom tat ung vien, lua chon interviewer va du lieu slot da ban

`CreateInterviewAsync(CreateInterviewRequest request)`
- kiem tra tinh nhat quan giua application/candidate/job/interviewer
- dam bao ho so dang o giai doan co the len lich
- tao interview
- chuyen trang thai application sang `Interview` neu duoc phep

`UpdateInterviewStatusAsync(...)`
- xac thuc interview ton tai
- parse chuoi status
- persist thay doi trang thai

`DeleteInterviewAsync(...)`
- xoa interview dang duoc tracking

----------------------------------------------------------
4. LOGIC SAP LICH
----------------------------------------------------------

`GetScheduleDataAsync(...)` va `BuildBusySlotsByDateAsync()` la trung tam cua trai nghiem sap lich.

Lua chon thiet ke quan trong:
- phan cong interviewer hien chua duoc persist chi tiet
- vi vay service tra ra ban do occupied-slot toan cuc nhu mot heuristic an toan

Dieu nay da duoc ghi chu trong code va rat quan trong de hieu gioi han hien tai cua module sap lich.

----------------------------------------------------------
5. LUAT NGHIEP VU
----------------------------------------------------------

`CreateInterviewAsync(...)` dang ep:
- application id phai hop le
- application phai ton tai
- application phai o `ManagerReview` hoac da o `Interview`
- candidate id neu co phai khop owner cua application
- job id neu co phai khop job cua application
- interviewer id neu co phai ton tai

----------------------------------------------------------
6. CAC HELPER
----------------------------------------------------------

`BuildBusySlotsByDateAsync()`
- tai interview da len lich trong 2 thang toi
- nhom theo ngay
- doi thoi gian interview sang so phut tinh tu dau ngay

`CloneBusySlots(...)`
- deep-copy dictionary de moi interviewer DTO co danh sach rieng

`BuildMeta(...)`
- helper phan trang tong quat

`ParseInterviewStatus(...)`
- chuan hoa nhieu nhan nhu `canceled` / `cancelled`

`MapInterviewListItem(...)`
- bien doi entity thanh dong du lieu cho danh sach UI

----------------------------------------------------------
7. MAU CO THE TAI SU DUNG
----------------------------------------------------------

`BuildMeta(...)` dang lap lai o nhieu application service va nen duoc tach thanh helper phan trang dung chung.
