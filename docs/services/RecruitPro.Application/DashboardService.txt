==========================================================
DashboardService - Phan Tich Chi Tiet
==========================================================

1. TONG QUAN
----------------------------------------------------------

DashboardService chuan bi payload dashboard cho 3 doi tuong:
- Candidate
- HR
- Manager

No khong phai service pass-through don thuan tu repository.
Service nay lam giau va tai cau truc du lieu thanh DTO than thien cho card va widget.

----------------------------------------------------------
2. PHU THUOC
----------------------------------------------------------

- `ICandidateProfileRepository`
- `IApplicationRepository`
- `IJobRepository`
- `IInterviewRepository`
- `INotificationRepository`

----------------------------------------------------------
3. DASHBOARD CHO CANDIDATE
----------------------------------------------------------

`GetCandidateDashboardAsync(Guid userId)`

Hanh vi chinh:
- tai profile ung vien
- tai tat ca ho so cua ung vien
- lay cac job da duoc approved va goi y dua tren vi tri hien tai va skill
- tinh toan thong ke
- chon buoi interview gan nhat trong tuong lai

Nhung vung output chinh:
- ten chao mung
- thong ke application/interview/notification
- card interview tiep theo
- danh sach job goi y

----------------------------------------------------------
4. DASHBOARD CHO HR
----------------------------------------------------------

`GetHrDashboardAsync()`

Hanh vi chinh:
- tai buoi interview tiep theo
- tai ho so moi nhat
- tai job dang cho duyet
- tinh cac thong ke van hanh co ban

Chi tiet dang chu y:
- mot so muc nhu hiring velocity va diversity report van dang mock/static
- service da mo hop dong UI du du lieu chua hoan toan dong

----------------------------------------------------------
5. DASHBOARD CHO MANAGER
----------------------------------------------------------

`GetManagerDashboardAsync()`

Hanh vi chinh:
- tai job dang cho duyet
- tai manager review queue
- tai thong ke so luong ho so theo trang thai
- tai metric review-cycle theo department
- tai danh sach department

Sau do tinh:
- pending approvals
- active applications
- average review cycle
- acceptance rate
- cac muc final-decision
- hiring speed theo department
- recruitment funnel

----------------------------------------------------------
6. CAC HELPER NOI BO
----------------------------------------------------------

`GetProfileEntityAsync(...)`
- helper truy van profile dung chung, nem `NotFoundException` khi khong co profile

`BuildPendingApprovalMeta(Job job)`
- tao chuoi department + salary label ngan gon cho card

`BuildManagerFunnel(...)`
- chuyen thong ke trang thai ho so thanh cac dong funnel

----------------------------------------------------------
7. GHI CHU VE KHA NANG DUNG CHUNG
----------------------------------------------------------

Pattern truy van profile dang bi lap lai o nhieu service:
- `DashboardService`
- `ApplicationService`
- `CandidateService`

Day la ung vien tot cho mot helper truy cap candidate-profile dung chung.
