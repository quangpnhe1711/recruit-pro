==========================================================
PdfResumeTextExtractor - Phan Tich Chi Tiet
==========================================================

1. TONG QUAN
----------------------------------------------------------

PdfResumeTextExtractor la implementation ha tang cua `IResumeTextExtractor`.

Nhiem vu cua no don gian:
- nhan mot stream PDF
- doc tat ca cac trang
- noi text da trich xuat
- tra ve plain text cho parsing downstream hoac AI enrichment

----------------------------------------------------------
2. PHU THUOC
----------------------------------------------------------

Thu vien ngoai:
- `UglyToad.PdfPig`

Phu thuoc noi bo:
- khong co ngoai stream dau vao va cancellation token

----------------------------------------------------------
3. LUONG CUA METHOD
----------------------------------------------------------

`ExtractTextAsync(Stream resumeStream, CancellationToken cancellationToken)`

Tung buoc:
1. validate stream khong null
2. dua vi tri stream ve dau neu stream cho phep seek
3. mo tai lieu PDF tu stream
4. duyet qua tat ca cac trang
5. kiem tra cancellation giua cac trang
6. append text tung trang vao `StringBuilder`
7. trim output cuoi va tra ve

----------------------------------------------------------
4. GHI CHU HANH VI
----------------------------------------------------------

- Method nay chi async o method signature; cong viec doc PDF ben trong la dong bo
- Cancellation duoc ho tro theo kieu cooperative trong luc duyet trang
- Method gia dinh stream dau vao la PDF hop le
- PDF khong co layer text co the van cho output rat kem tuy chat luong tai lieu

----------------------------------------------------------
5. CALLER
----------------------------------------------------------

Nhung noi dang dung:
- `CandidateService.ExtractResumeTextAsync`
- `CopilotService.EnrichPoolWithResumeTextAsync`

Trong ca hai luong, extractor nay la primitive nen tang de bien file CV
thanh text co the tim kiem hoac cho AI doc duoc.

----------------------------------------------------------
6. Y TUONG CAI TIEN
----------------------------------------------------------

- Them OCR fallback cho PDF scan neu sau nay can
- Them file type sniffing neu gioi thieu nhieu extractor sau mot dispatcher chung
