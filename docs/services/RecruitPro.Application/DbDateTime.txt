==========================================================
DbDateTime - Phan Tich Chi Tiet
==========================================================

1. TONG QUAN
----------------------------------------------------------

`DbDateTime` la mot utility chung nho duoc dung xuyen suot trong cac application service.

Muc dich cua no la chuan hoa gia tri ngay gio ghi vao domain layer,
nhat la khi codebase muon tranh viec lech `DateTimeKind`.

----------------------------------------------------------
2. CAC THANH VIEN DUOC MO RA
----------------------------------------------------------

`Now`
- tra ve ngay gio hien tai theo local time
- dong thoi gan ro `DateTimeKind.Unspecified`

`Today`
- tra ve phan ngay cua `Now`

`CurrentYear`
- tra ve `Now.Year`

----------------------------------------------------------
3. LY DO TON TAI
----------------------------------------------------------

Du an thuong xuyen ghi cac moc thoi gian nhu:
- `CreatedAt`
- `UpdatedAt`
- `AppliedAt`
- `SentAt`
- `UploadDate`

Dung mot utility dung chung giup giam cac loi goi truc tiep `DateTime.Now`
trong service nghiep vu va giu hanh vi persist on dinh hon.

----------------------------------------------------------
4. NHUNG NOI DANG SU DUNG
----------------------------------------------------------

Helper nay duoc dung o nhieu service nhu:
- `AuthService`
- `CandidateService`
- `ApplicationService`
- `DashboardService`
- `InterviewService`
- `OfferService`
- `ManagerAnalyticsService`

----------------------------------------------------------
5. Y TUONG CAI TIEN
----------------------------------------------------------

- Neu he thong sau nay chuyen sang UTC toan bo, day la diem trung tam de doi chinh sach
- Neu can test tot hon, utility nay co the tien hoa thanh abstraction clock co the inject
