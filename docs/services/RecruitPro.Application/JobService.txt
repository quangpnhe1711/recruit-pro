==========================================================
JobService - Phan Tich Chi Tiet
==========================================================

1. TONG QUAN
----------------------------------------------------------

JobService la tang dieu phoi chinh cho cac luong nghiep vu lien quan den job.

No phuc vu ca use case huong candidate va quan tri noi bo:
- danh sach/tim kiem job cong khai
- man hinh chi tiet job
- quan ly job cho HR
- hang doi/chi tiet duyet cho manager
- tao, patch, cap nhat status va xoa job

----------------------------------------------------------
2. PHU THUOC
----------------------------------------------------------

- `IJobRepository`
- `IApplicationRepository`
- `ISkillRepository`
- `IUnitOfWork`
- `IMapper`

----------------------------------------------------------
3. CAC METHOD HUONG CANDIDATE
----------------------------------------------------------

`GetJobsAsync(...)`
- tra ve job da approved co phan trang don gian
- dung AutoMapper cho card job

`SearchJobsAsync(JobQueryRequest request)`
- chuyen bo loc tim kiem xuong repository
- map ket qua sang `JobListItemDto`

`GetFiltersAsync()`
- tra ve bucket salary tinh san va ten skill dong

`GetDepartmentsAsync()`
- mo danh muc department

`GetSkillsAsync()`
- mo danh muc skill

`GetJobDetailAsync(string jobId)`
- tra ve DTO chi tiet cu

`GetJobScreenDetailAsync(string jobId)`
- tra ve DTO man hinh day du hon, gom thong ke application va skill co cau truc

`GetJobStatisticsAsync(string jobId)`
- tra ve so lieu funnel tuyen dung cua mot job

----------------------------------------------------------
4. CAC METHOD NOI BO / CHO HR
----------------------------------------------------------

`UpdateJobStatusAsync(...)`
- xac thuc enum duoc yeu cau
- cap nhat truc tiep job dang duoc track

`GetHrJobsAsync(...)`
- danh sach phan trang cho HR voi bo loc department/status/creator
- dong thoi tinh thong ke tu toan bo tap ket qua phu hop

`GetManagerApprovalQueueAsync(...)`
- tra ve card cho hang doi pending approval va cac summary counter

`GetManagerApprovalDetailAsync(...)`
- tra ve trang review day du cho manager
- gom metadata job, owner info, skills, pipeline insights va thong tin phe duyet

`CreateJobAsync(...)`
- resolve department
- xay dung danh sach skill da chuan hoa
- serialize cac truong dang list
- tao job o trang thai pending approval

`PatchJobAsync(...)`
- diem vao update tung phan
- chi cap nhat truong duoc gui len
- co the thay toan bo tap skill

`DeleteJobAsync(...)`
- tai doi tuong dang track roi hard delete

----------------------------------------------------------

----------------------------------------------------------
6. NHAN XET THIET KE
----------------------------------------------------------

- Service nay dang tron concern tim kiem cong khai, CRUD cho HR va approval cho manager trong mot class
- Phan lap rap DTO kha lon va mang dam dau an UI
- Nhieu helper la pure va co the tai su dung

Class nay la ung vien ro rang de tach trong tuong lai:
- `PublicJobQueryService`
- `HrJobManagementService`
- `ManagerJobApprovalService`

----------------------------------------------------------
7. GHI CHU VE KHA NANG DUNG CHUNG
----------------------------------------------------------

Nhung helper dung chung tiem nang:
- helper tao metadata phan trang
- helper parse/serialize mang chuoi JSON
- helper format salary label
- helper lookup job tracked/untracked
