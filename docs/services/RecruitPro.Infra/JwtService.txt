==========================================================
JwtService - Phan Tich Chi Tiet
==========================================================

1. TONG QUAN
----------------------------------------------------------

JwtService la implementation ha tang cua `IJwtService`.

Trach nhiem cua no rat tap trung:
- tao access token va refresh token cho user da xac thuc
- nhung claim danh tinh va role vao token
- su dung issuer, audience, signing key va thoi gian het han duoc cau hinh

Service nay khong xac thuc credential.
Buoc authentication duoc xu ly som hon boi `AuthService`.

----------------------------------------------------------
2. PHU THUOC
----------------------------------------------------------

`JwtSettings`

Nhung field duoc su dung:
- `Key`
- `Issuer`
- `Audience`
- `ExpiryMinutes`
- `RefreshTokenExpiryMinutes`

Service nay phu thuoc hoan toan vao do dung cua cau hinh.

----------------------------------------------------------
3. PHUONG THUC CHINH
----------------------------------------------------------

`GenerateToken(User user, string type)`

Muc dich:
- tao chuoi JWT da ky cho user duoc truyen vao

Luong chinh:
1. tao cac claim co ban
- `sub`
- `nameidentifier`
- `email`
- `jti`

2. them role claims
- moi role trong `user.UserRoles` tro thanh mot `ClaimTypes.Role`

3. tao signing key
- dung symmetric HMAC SHA256

4. chon thoi gian het han theo loai token
- `"Access"` dung `ExpiryMinutes`
- cac gia tri khac fallback sang `RefreshTokenExpiryMinutes`

5. tao `JwtSecurityToken`

6. serialize token thanh string bang `JwtSecurityTokenHandler`

----------------------------------------------------------
4. THIET KE CLAIM
----------------------------------------------------------

Claim danh tinh bao gom:
- User id trong ca `sub` va `nameidentifier`
- Email de tra cuu danh tinh xuong downstream
- `jti` ngau nhien de dam bao tinh duy nhat

Claim phan quyen bao gom:
- tat ca role duoc gan cho user

Dieu nay cho phep authorization downstream dua truc tiep vao role claim trong JWT
ma khong can doc lai role tu database o cac request thong thuong.

----------------------------------------------------------
5. GHI CHU QUAN TRONG
----------------------------------------------------------

- Service gia dinh `user.UserRoles` va `Role.Name` da duoc load san
- Token type dang dung string thay vi enum, nen caller phai dong nhat
- Thoi gian het han dang dung `DateTime.Now`, khong dung `DbDateTime.Now`
- Khong co luu tru refresh token o day; service nay chi tao token da ky

----------------------------------------------------------
6. TUONG TAC
----------------------------------------------------------

Caller chinh:
- `AuthService`

Cach dung dien hinh:
- `LoginCoreAsync` tao ca access token va refresh token
- sau do AutoMapper dat chung vao `LoginResponseDto`

----------------------------------------------------------
7. RUI RO / Y TUONG CAI TIEN
----------------------------------------------------------

- Thay chuoi `"Access"` bang enum hoac constants class ro rang
- Can nhac gom logic tao claim JWT neu sau nay co them service phat hanh token
- Can nhac chuyen sang `DateTime.UtcNow` neu he thong chuan hoa xu ly token theo UTC
