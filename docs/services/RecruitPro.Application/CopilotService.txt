==========================================================
CopilotService - Phan Tich Chi Tiet
==========================================================

1. TONG QUAN
----------------------------------------------------------

CopilotService la service dieu phoi o tang application cho tinh nang recruitment copilot.

No quan ly:
- hoi thoai copilot
- nap candidate pool
- xep hang xac dinh
- tinh chinh xep hang bang AI khi can
- hoi dap kieu chat cho recruiter
- tap rule screening da luu
- lich su ranking session

Day la mot trong nhung service giau tinh nang nhat cua he thong.

----------------------------------------------------------
2. PHU THUOC
----------------------------------------------------------

- `ICopilotRepository`
- `IFileStorageService`
- `IResumeTextExtractor`
- `IAiCopilotProvider`
- `IUnitOfWork`
- `OpenAiSettings`

----------------------------------------------------------
3. CAC CHE DO HOAT DONG CAP CAO
----------------------------------------------------------

Service van hanh theo 2 che do:

1. Chat mode
- recruiter dat cau hoi bang ngon ngu tu nhien
- khong can ranking
- AI tra ve cau tra loi ngan gon

2. Ranking mode
- rule/prompt yeu cau xep hang thuc su
- service tinh ket qua xac dinh truoc
- AI co the tinh chinh hoac giai thich them

----------------------------------------------------------
4. CAC PHUONG THUC CONG KHAI
----------------------------------------------------------

`GetJobsAsync()`
- tra ve danh sach job co the chon khi vao copilot

`CreateConversationAsync(...)`
- tai su dung hoi thoai moi nhat cung job/user neu co
- neu khong thi tao hoi thoai active moi

`GetConversationAsync(...)`
- dam bao dung owner truoc khi tra ve message va metadata

`GetCandidatePoolAsync(Guid jobId)`
- tai snapshot candidate pool cua mot job

`CreateRankingAsync(...)`
- workflow copilot cot loi
- quyet dinh chay chat mode hay ranking mode

`GetRankingSessionAsync(...)`
- tra ve chi tiet ranking session trong lich su

`GetSavedRulesAsync(...)`
- tra ve saved rule cua user cho mot job

`CreateSavedRuleAsync(...)`
- chuan hoa rules tu criteria va persist

`UpdateSavedRuleStatusAsync(...)`
- bat/tat trang thai active cua saved rule

`DeleteSavedRuleAsync(...)`
- soft-delete mot saved rule

----------------------------------------------------------
5. LUONG TAO XEP HANG
----------------------------------------------------------

`CreateRankingAsync(...)` la noi chua logic dieu phoi quan trong nhat.

Nhung giai doan chinh:
1. validate quyen so huu hoi thoai va tinh nhat quan job
2. tai candidate pool
3. lam giau pool bang resume text neu co the
4. quyet dinh di theo nhanh chat hay ranking

Nhanh chat:
- goi `TryCreateChatReplyAsync`
- persist message cua user va assistant
- cap nhat timestamp hoi thoai

Nhanh ranking:
- tai cac saved rule dang active
- chuan hoa rules tu prompt + criteria + saved rules
- tuy chon gop them ngu canh tu ranking session gan nhat
- tinh xep hang xac dinh
- goi AI de tinh chinh/giai thich ket qua
- chuan hoa ket qua cuoi
- persist message hoi thoai
- persist ranking session
- cap nhat con tro ranking moi nhat cua hoi thoai

----------------------------------------------------------
6. TANG XEP HANG XAC DINH
----------------------------------------------------------

Nhom helper quan trong:
- `BuildRules(...)`
- `RankCandidates(...)`
- `GetPenaltyScore(...)`
- `ShouldRunRanking(...)`
- `NormalizeRankingResults(...)`
- `MergeRules(...)`
- `MatchesAutoReject(...)`
- `IsNegativeHit(...)`

Nhung helper nay cho thay he thong khong phu thuoc hoan toan vao AI de tao ranking dung duoc.
AI la lop bo tro, khong phai dong co duy nhat.

----------------------------------------------------------
7. LAM GIAU NOI DUNG CV
----------------------------------------------------------

`EnrichPoolWithResumeTextAsync(...)`

Muc dich:
- tang chat luong ranking va chat bang cach bo sung text trich xuat tu PDF vao tom tat CV

Hanh vi:
- tai tung CV khi co URL
- trich xuat text bang `IResumeTextExtractor`
- cat ngan theo gioi han ky tu cau hinh
- bo qua im lang moi loi trich xuat

No duoc thiet ke theo kieu best-effort de copilot van chay du resume parsing that bai.

----------------------------------------------------------
8. HELPER MAPPING / SERIALIZATION
----------------------------------------------------------

Service nay chua nhieu helper mapping luu tru:
- `MapConversation(...)`
- `MapConversationDetail(...)`
- `MapRankingSession(...)`
- `MapSavedRule(...)`
- `MapCriterion(...)`
- `DeserializeRules(...)`
- `DeserializeStringList(...)`
- `DeserializeSummary(...)`

Nhung helper nay giu repository don gian hon bang cach luu JSON roi tai tao DTO typed o tang service.

----------------------------------------------------------
9. GHI CHU VE KHA NANG DUNG CHUNG
----------------------------------------------------------

Nhung huong tach co the xem xet:
- utility serialize/deserialize JSON cho metadata copilot
- helper OpenAI-compatible dung chung
- primitive chuan hoa ranking-rule co the tai su dung
