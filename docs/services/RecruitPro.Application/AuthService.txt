==========================================================
AuthService - Phan Tich Chi Tiet
==========================================================

1. TONG QUAN
----------------------------------------------------------

AuthService xu ly hanh vi dang nhap va quen mat khau cho ca ung vien
va nguoi dung noi bo.

Trach nhiem chinh:
- xac thuc cap email/password
- ep dung luat role theo tung portal
- tao access token va refresh token
- cap mat khau tam cho luong quen mat khau

Service nay co logic luu tru khong phuc tap va uy quyen viec tao token
cho `JwtService`.

----------------------------------------------------------
2. PHU THUOC
----------------------------------------------------------

- `IUserRepository`
- `IJwtService`
- `IEmailService`
- `IUnitOfWork`
- `IMapper`

Hang so ho tro:
- URL dang nhap cho candidate
- URL dang nhap cho internal

----------------------------------------------------------
3. CAC DIEM VAO CONG KHAI
----------------------------------------------------------

`LoginAsync(LoginRequest request)`
- duong dang nhap tong quat dung email/password tu request

`CandidateLoginAsync(string email, string password)`
- gioi han dang nhap cho user co role `Candidate`

`InternalLoginAsync(string employeeIdOrEmail, string password)`
- gioi han dang nhap cho cac role khac candidate

`ForgotCandidatePasswordAsync(string email)`
- cap mat khau tam khi tai khoan thuoc candidate

`ForgotInternalPasswordAsync(string employeeIdOrEmail)`
- cap mat khau tam khi tai khoan thuoc internal user

----------------------------------------------------------
4. LUONG NOI BO COT LOI
----------------------------------------------------------

`LoginCoreAsync(...)`

Hanh vi chinh:
1. tai user theo email
2. xac thuc hash mat khau bang BCrypt
3. tai danh sach role
4. ap bo loc role neu co
5. tao access token
6. tao refresh token
7. map user sang `LoginResponseDto`
8. gan token da tao thong qua AutoMapper context items

Method nay tap trung hoa logic xac thuc de cac public method chi con dinh nghia ranh gioi role.

----------------------------------------------------------
5. LUONG QUEN MAT KHAU
----------------------------------------------------------

`ResetPasswordAsync(...)`

Hanh vi quan trong:
- identifier rong tra ve bad request
- khong tim thay user van tra ve response chung giong thanh cong
- user sai role cung tra ve response chung do

Muc dich la tranh account enumeration.

Neu tai khoan hop le:
1. tao mat khau tam
2. bam hash bang BCrypt
3. cap nhat `UpdatedAt`
4. persist thay doi
5. goi email service voi portal URL phu hop

----------------------------------------------------------
6. GHI CHU BAO MAT
----------------------------------------------------------

- BCrypt duoc dung cho xac thuc va hash khi reset
- Su ton tai cua user duoc an trong response quen mat khau
- Ranh gioi role duoc kiem tra sau buoc verify mat khau
- Viec tao mat khau tam don gian nhung du de dung cho bootstrap/reset

----------------------------------------------------------
7. MAU PATTERN CO THE TAI SU DUNG
----------------------------------------------------------

Nhung helper co the tach chung:
- tao mat khau tam
- validate portal theo role
- chinh sach response chuan hoa cho forgot-password
