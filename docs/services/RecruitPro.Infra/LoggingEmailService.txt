==========================================================
LoggingEmailService - Phan Tich Chi Tiet
==========================================================

1. TONG QUAN
----------------------------------------------------------

LoggingEmailService la implementation nhe cua `IEmailService`.

No khong gui email that.
Thay vao do, no ghi lai y dinh gui email va payload vao logger cua ung dung.

Use case hien tai:
- email moi candidate sau khi import hang loat
- email reset mat khau cho candidate/internal portal

Day thuc chat la mot adapter stub an toan cho moi truong phat trien.

----------------------------------------------------------
2. PHU THUOC
----------------------------------------------------------

`ILogger<LoggingEmailService>`

Duoc dung de ghi:
- loai email
- nguoi nhan
- mat khau tam
- login URL

----------------------------------------------------------
3. CAC THAO TAC HO TRO
----------------------------------------------------------

`SendCandidateInvitationAsync(...)`

Muc dich:
- ghi nhan rang he thong muon gui email moi cho candidate moi duoc import

Thong tin duoc log:
- email nguoi nhan
- ho ten nguoi nhan
- mat khau tam da tao
- URL dang nhap muc tieu

`SendPasswordResetAsync(...)`

Muc dich:
- ghi nhan rang he thong muon gui email reset mat khau

Thong tin duoc log:
- email nguoi nhan
- ho ten nguoi nhan
- mat khau tam da tao
- URL dang nhap muc tieu

----------------------------------------------------------
4. DAC TINH HANH VI
----------------------------------------------------------

- Ca hai method deu khong chan luong nghiep vu o tang ha tang
- Ca hai method deu luon tra ve `Task.CompletedTask`
- Khong co tich hop SMTP/API
- Khong co retry logic
- Khong co template engine

----------------------------------------------------------
5. CALLER
----------------------------------------------------------

Nhung noi dang goi:
- `CandidateService.ImportCandidatesAsync`
- `AuthService.ResetPasswordAsync`

Vi service nay gan nhu khong nem loi trong dieu kien binh thuong, nhung luong tren duoc giu don gian trong luc dev va test local.

----------------------------------------------------------
6. KHI NAO SERVICE NAY DA DU
----------------------------------------------------------

Implementation nay phu hop khi:
- team van dang xay cac backend flow
- chuc nang gui email chua san sang production
- chi can log de xac minh nghiep vu dung

----------------------------------------------------------
7. KHOANG TRONG DE LEN PRODUCTION
----------------------------------------------------------

De dua vao production, service nay nhieu kha nang se duoc thay boi:
- SMTP adapter
- tich hop SendGrid/Mailgun/Postmark
- render HTML template
- theo doi trang thai delivery
- quan ly credential bang secret
